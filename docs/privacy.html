RMSNKRS eBay MCP Privacy Policy

This is a personal, local-use eBay integration operated by the account owner.

The application uses eBay OAuth only for the owner's account and only to perform eBay actions requested by the owner.

OAuth credentials are stored locally on the owner's computer. They are not sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or stored in a separate remote database.

The owner can revoke access at any time through eBay account settings.
